01 — Who We Are

Built by industry veterans. Rooted in Sarawak.

Bima Titanium Sdn. Bhd. was established by senior professionals who spent their careers working across oil & gas, petrochemical and industrial plant environments — most of it in and around Bintulu, the operational heart of Sarawak's LNG and petrochemical sector.

Operating from Bintulu puts us where the work happens. Being based directly alongside Sarawak's major plant operations means shorter mobilisation times, established local contractor relationships, and an operating rhythm shaped by the same shutdown and turnaround cycles our clients run on — not a contractor commuting in from outside the state.

We combine plant execution experience with contract, procurement and commercial discipline — giving clients a contractor that understands both how the work is performed and how it must be controlled.
